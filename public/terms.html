The fine print

Store terms.

Practical basics for browsing, ordering and using this store.

Last updated: October 2026. This is a general template, not legal advice. Have the final terms reviewed for the country and region where the store operates before accepting real customers.

Product information

We aim to show accurate product descriptions, images, prices and stock. Product photos may not perfectly represent colour, texture or scale on every screen. Inventory is checked again when an order is submitted.

Orders

Submitting an order records a request to purchase the listed items. The store may contact the customer to clarify delivery details or cancel an order if an item cannot be supplied. An order reference is generated after successful creation.

Prices and delivery

Prices are displayed in Pakistani rupees. Shipping is Rs. 250 for orders below Rs. 5,000 and free for orders at or above that threshold. The checkout summary is the source of truth for the amount recorded for an order.

Payment

Cash on delivery is the only payment method enabled in this starter. No online payment is taken through this website. Do not represent online card payments as available until a real payment provider integration has been tested.

Accounts

You are responsible for keeping your account credentials private and for contacting the store if you believe someone else has accessed your account. We may suspend accounts used to abuse the service.

Returns and support

A clear, business-specific return and refund policy must be added before a public launch. Contact support with your order reference to ask about an order.

Changes

These terms may need to be updated when products, delivery coverage, payment methods or applicable requirements change.
