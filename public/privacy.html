Privacy notice.

How this store uses the information needed to provide shopping and account services.

Last updated: October 2026. This is a starter privacy notice and must be reviewed and adapted to your business, jurisdiction, hosting arrangements, and actual practices before public launch.

Information you provide

When you create an account, we store your name, email address and a password hash. Orders may include delivery details such as recipient name, email, phone, address and order notes. Contact forms and newsletter subscriptions store the details submitted in those forms.

Passwords and sessions

Passwords are hashed using Argon2id; the original password is not saved. Session identifiers are stored in an HTTP-only cookie and server-side session data is stored in SQLite. Do not reuse passwords from other services.

Orders and payments

This build supports cash on delivery. It does not collect card numbers or process online payments. If a payment provider is added in the future, this notice must be updated to explain that provider's role.

Local browser storage

For visitors who have not signed in, this website saves a guest cart and guest wishlist in the browser's local storage. These are shopping preferences, not authentication credentials. After sign-in, the website attempts to merge them into the account.

How information is used

Information is used to operate accounts, create and manage orders, maintain inventory, respond to contact messages, and process newsletter subscriptions. Do not collect information that is not necessary for those purposes.

Retention and deletion

Records remain in the store database until the operator removes them according to a documented retention policy. The present starter build does not include a customer self-service account deletion feature; add and test that workflow before production launch.

Security and contact

The application includes password hashing, session cookies, CSRF checks, rate limiting and request validation. No website can guarantee absolute security. Configure HTTPS, access-controlled backups, patching, monitoring and a secure database host before launch. Use the contact page for privacy questions.
