The useful details

Good to know.

Answers to common questions about shopping with SMH Collections.

How do I place an order?
Choose a product, add it to your bag and continue to checkout. You'll need to sign in or create an account to place the order. Prices, stock and shipping are checked by the server.
Which payment methods are available?
Cash on delivery is currently enabled. Online card payments are not active in this version of the store, and SMH Collections does not collect card numbers.
How much is shipping?
Shipping is Rs. 250 on orders below Rs. 5,000. Orders of Rs. 5,000 or more qualify for free shipping. The final amount is shown before the order is placed.
Can I track my order?
Sign in and open Order history to view order references and the latest status recorded by the store.
Can I cancel an order?
Contact the store as soon as possible with your order reference. Cancellation depends on the current status; the administrator can cancel an order before it is shipped.
How do I reset my password?
Open the account page and use “Forgot your password?”. In local development, the reset link is printed in the server terminal. A live deployment needs valid outgoing email settings.
Why does checkout ask me to sign in?
Orders are linked to an account so you can review their status later. Items added before sign-in are merged into your account cart after you log in or register.
How can I contact you?
Use our contact form. Submissions are saved in the store database for the team to review. Contact SMH Collections →
